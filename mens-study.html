Faithful Before Found

A five-week Bible study for men who want to lead well in dating and, one day, in marriage. Not by taking control, but by learning to serve first and becoming a man whose word holds.

For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.

Start week 1

How it works

Each week has a theme, a key verse, and one practice to live out. Set aside 30 minutes a day for six days, then take day 7 to look back and go deeper. Every passage is printed on the page in the King James Version, so all you need is this site and a notebook.

Read

Read the day’s passage slowly, twice. About 10 to 15 minutes.

Answer

Three quick questions in your notebook to lock in the thought. About 5 minutes.

Pray and act

Pray the passage back to God and choose one specific action for today. About 10 minutes.

On day 7, reread your six daily answers and work through the week’s deeper journal questions.

Don’t do it alone

Before you start, pick one man you trust: a friend, a pastor, or a mentor. Send him a short update at the end of every week. Consistency is far easier when someone expects to hear from you.
